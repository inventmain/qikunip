Founded on Top-Tier Standards, Dedicated to Client Value

Our journey is one of continuous evolution toward patent value realization. We never stop at merely securing paper certificates; we transform patents into formidable commercial weapons and revenue-generating liquid assets.

2010 - 2017: Foundation · Hanhao IP

Founded by Mr. Luo Peng, former partner at King & Wood Mallesons (KWM). We infused rigorous top-tier law firm processes, elite standards, and global vision into our institutional DNA, establishing an unshakeable professional foundation.

2017 - 2022: Expansion · QiKun IP

Succeeded all operations of Hanhao and upgraded under the brand "QiKun" (CNIPA Agency Code: 11655). Serving hundreds of innovative enterprises, we accumulated profound industry insights to proactively engineer and manage corporate IP portfolios.

2023 - Present: Focus · Patent Monetization Experts

Pioneered an institutional transformation toward patent monetization and high-stakes litigation defense. Full focus on patent licensing, transaction execution, litigation funding models, and monetization-grade claim engineering.
