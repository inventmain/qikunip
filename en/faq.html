About Patent Monetization

Patent valuation depends on technical relevance, claim validity, market demand, competitor footprint, and demonstrable evidence of infringement. QiKun combines AI big-data screening with rigorous review by our senior Beijing team (former CNIPA examiners and seasoned trial attorneys) to produce a comprehensive valuation report. Initial triage is conducted at no cost.

The primary models are direct divestment (outright asset sales), licensing programs (granting rights in exchange for ongoing royalties), and patent assertion/litigation (recovering damages upon infringement). QiKun’s experts assess your portfolio dynamics and commercial goals to architect a tailored monetization pathway.

About High-Value Patent Prosecution

Standard patent filing merely aims to obtain a certificate; high-value prosecution aims to engineer monetizable commercial assets. Starting with the end in mind—competitive blocking and future transactions—we draft claim sets that establish formidable technical moats and withstand litigation-level invalidation challenges.

An isolated patent is easily designed around by competitors. A meticulously architected patent cluster creates multi-layered protection spanning fundamental mechanisms, peripheral implementations, and substitute embodiments—establishing an impenetrable technical moat critical for commercial dominance and high-value transactions.

About Patent Consulting & Invalidation Defense

This is precisely the purpose of Freedom to Operate (FTO) clearance. QiKun deconstructs your product's technical architecture, executes exhaustive worldwide patent searches, maps all claim elements, and delivers formal legal clearance opinions—ensuring safe market entry and mitigating costly litigation risks.

Patent invalidation petitions are common tactical maneuvers in commercial competition. Upon receiving CNIPA's notice, prompt response is essential. QiKun’s litigators (including veterans of landmark patent disputes such as the Viagra patent defense) dissect the petitioner's prior art combinations, draft rigorous rebuttal arguments, and fiercely defend your claim scope in oral hearings.
