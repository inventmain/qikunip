Conventional Agency vs. QiKun: Two Distinct Destinations

The commercial destiny of a patent is largely sealed the moment the application is filed.

Conventional Agency

  • Objective: Secure a grant certificate
  • Perspective: Purely technical—passively translating engineering disclosures
  • Process: Reactive drafting—writing only what is handed over
  • Deliverable: A nominal certificate—often vulnerable to design-arounds and difficult to license or monetize
  • Outcome: Patents remain an expense / cost center

QiKun Intellectual Property

  • Objective: Engineer high-value commercial assets
  • Perspective: Commercial & defensive value—identifying decisive competitive blocking points
  • Process: Proactive architecture—restructuring claim hierarchy into layered defensive clusters
  • Deliverable: An actionable patent portfolio asset package with validated monetization pathways
  • Outcome: Patents become a strategic corporate profit center

Global Patent Strategy: Extending Territorial Value Worldwide

In today's integrated global economy, domestic-only protection is inherently vulnerable. We architect cost-optimized international filing roadmaps to expand your technological moats across key global jurisdictions.

PCT International Route

"One application, global reach." Leveraging the PCT system, a single filing defers national phase entry costs across 150+ member states by up to 30/31 months—securing 18 months of critical runway for market testing and capital allocation.

Paris Convention Route

Ideal for targeted jurisdictions requiring rapid direct entry. By invoking 12-month priority rights, you file directly into select overseas patent offices with maximum strategic speed and targeted focus.
