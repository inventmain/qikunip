Unique Challenges of High-Value Patent Transactions

Transactions involving SEPs and litigation-grade patents differ fundamentally from conventional patent listings. High sensitivity and systemic market exposure render traditional open-auction brokerages wholly unsuitable.

Challenges for Asset Holders (Sellers)

  • The Inability to Publicly Advertise
    High-value patents involve sensitive strategic assets. Sellers cannot publicly market their intent without alerting rivals, making qualified buyer discovery exceedingly difficult.
  • Protracted Deal Horizon & Attrition
    Bilateral SEP negotiations span months or years. Without structured brokerage frameworks, transactions frequently collapse due to communication friction and asymmetric expectations.
  • Prohibitive Counterparty Trust Costs
    Buyers and sellers are frequently direct industry competitors. Direct bilateral approaches trigger strategic suspicion and competitive pushback that derail trust.

Challenges for Prospective Acquirers (Buyers)

  • Sourcing Obscured Tier-1 Assets
    Truly decisive patent assets are rarely listed publicly. Acquirers struggle to identify high-probability acquisition targets through standard commercial channels.
  • Technical & Legal Due Diligence Complexity
    Evaluating essentiality and litigation claim strength demands deep technical reverse engineering. Buyers cannot rely on superficial claim text alone.
  • Strategic Intent Exposure Risk
    Direct overtures to sellers broadcast corporate technology roadmaps and defensive vulnerabilities, empowering counterparties to hike prices or trigger pre-emptive lawsuits.
