How Does Full Contingency Representation Eliminate All Obstacles?

In traditional models, every monetization milestone brings severe uncertainty and prohibitive legal expense. QiKun’s contingency model shifts all risks to us.

Step 1: Complimentary Valuation (Zero Cost)

You Provide: Patent numbers or technical disclosures.
We Deliver: AI-powered claim screening paired with expert review to identify high-probability monetization targets.

Step 2: Strategy Alignment & Retainer (Zero Risk)

You Provide: Alignment on commercial goals and acceptable settlement parameters.
We Deliver: Tailored execution plan and formal contingency agreement. You incur zero upfront fees.

Step 3: Full-Scale Execution (We Invest)

We Deliver: QiKun commits all technical capital, claim chart reverse engineering, buyer outreach, and legal expenses, providing transparent milestone reports throughout.

Step 4: Realized Liquidity & Shared Upside (Shared Success)

You Receive: The majority share of cash divestment proceeds, running royalties, or litigation damage awards.
We Receive: An agreed performance-based contingency fee upon successful closing.
